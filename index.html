ABOUT

Two sides of the same skill

I'm a recent Computer Science (AI-ML) graduate from Sir Padampat Singhania University, based in Hyderabad. Over four internships I've spent roughly equal time on two things that most people treat as separate jobs: reading data well enough to tell a business what to do next, and training models well enough to let a system decide on its own.

As a Data Analyst, I've worked across 50,000+ transaction records and 9,500+ restaurant listings building KPI dashboards, star-schema models, and a Random Forest forecast at 92%+ accuracy. As an ML Engineer, I've shipped a real-time hand-gesture recognition system and a brain-tumor MRI classifier, both north of 93% accuracy.

What I actually enjoy is the seam between the two building the pipeline that gets messy data into a model, and the dashboard that explains what the model just did.
